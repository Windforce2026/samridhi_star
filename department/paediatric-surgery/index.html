Paediatric Surgery

Home Departments Paediatric Surgery

We are aware that youngsters need particular attention, particularly in terms of their health. For this reason, the paediatric surgery department at Samridhi Hospital Siliguri is committed to giving kids of all ages—from newborns to teenagers—the best possible medical care. We give our word that every child who walks through our doors receives the best care in a kind and caring manner. All thanks to our staff of highly qualified physicians and surgeons. Children have distinct health demands that call for an all-exclusive care, they are not just tiny grownups. Our physicians in Siliguri have extensive training and experience in treating a variety of paediatric health conditions. Whether it's an intricate surgical operation or a standard check-up, we are equipped to handle it with utmost care and precision.

One of the top focus areas in our department is providing regular healthcare. We can track a child's development and growth with regular checks. Early detection of health issues improves the effectiveness of treatment. Our paediatrician takes immense time to get to know the history of the child in order to deliver personalized treatment procedure. From health screenings and vaccinations to growth and developmental evaluations, we offer all-inclusive services to keep kids healthy

Nevertheless, the risks are significantly greater when a youngster needs surgery. Parents want to know that the safest hands are handling their children. We at Samridhi Hospital Siliguri take great pride and satisfaction in our paediatricians who are dedicated to providing every kid with the best treatment possible.

For any parent, the thought of surgery can be frightening, but at Samridhi Hospital, we go above and beyond to make the process go as smoothly and stress-free as possible. With the most up-to-date medical equipment, our paediatric surgery department can provide youngsters with cutting-edge treatment alternatives. .

Our paediatricians in Siliguri are skilled at working with children and know how to put them at ease. We take care to calm the youngster and their parents by explaining procedures in a soothing manner. The aim is to reduce discomfort and anxiety during the process. For each and every child who walk inside our doors, our surgeons, nurses and support staff collaborate closely to create a cozy and warm environment for the child.

We provide more than just surgical solutions at Samridhi Hospital. We support offering all-encompassing care that attends to a child's medical needs. We provide a wide range of services which makes sure the children stay happy and healthy. We are here to support your child at every stage, from basic immunisations, health screening to management of chronic diseases.

We are here to provide top-notch care for your child, from infancy through adolescence, and to support you in keeping your child healthy and happy. Whether it’s for a routine check-up or a surgical procedure, you can trust Samridhi Hospital to deliver the best care for your child’s well-being.

Our goal is to create a positive, supportive environment for both children and their families. At Samridhi Hospital, we’re committed to providing exceptional paediatric care that ensures your child has a bright and healthy future ahead.

What are the conditions treated by a paediatric surgeon?

Paediatric surgeon specializes in treating major health issues of a child such as Adrenal Cancer, Crohn’s Disease, Colon Polyps, Conjoined Twins and many more.

How long does it take to recover from the surgery?

The recovery from the surgery depends on the procedure's health condition of the patient. Minor surgeries have a recovery period of 3 to 4 days while the recovery time of complex surgeries may last from a few months to a whole year.

How is paediatric surgery different from adult surgery?

Paediatric surgery is only performed on children below the age of 18, these surgeries are conducted by professionals who have expertise with minimally invasive procedures, anaesthesia, and managing the needs of the children.

What is the risk associated with paediatric surgery?

Loss of blood, side effects of anaesthesia, and infection are some of the potential risks of paediatric surgery. Fortunately, due to advancements in technology most of these risks are easily managed by the surgeons leading to positive results.

Can I stay with my child during surgery?

No, we don’t allow parents to stay inside the operation theatre during surgery. Allowing non-medical individuals into the operating room is illegal and is against the ethical guidelines.

How long does the surgery take?

The time depends on the complexity and the procedure of the surgery. Before entering the operation theatre the surgeon will discuss with you the process and duration of the surgery.
